02

Set your starting lineup

Start at MP level one, anger zero and five stages above zero. Resolve Double Power, alignment, Tokui-Waza and Sensei swaps before play.

03

Seven steps. One turn.

  1. Draw

    Active player draws three.

  2. Non-Combat

    Play eligible Allies, Dragon Balls, Non-Combat cards, Drills and locations.

  3. Power Up

    Raise MP stages by PUR, plus one for Tokui-Waza; Allies gain one. Respect maximum stages.

  4. Declare

    Choose Combat or skip it. Playing a Battleground or Location requires skipping Combat.

  5. Combat

    Resolve entering-Combat effects, active player first. Opponent draws three. Alternate actions until consecutive passes.

  6. Discard

    Both players reduce their hands to at most one card.

  7. Rejuvenation

    If you skipped Combat, optionally return your top discard to the Life Deck’s bottom.

Use Next step to advance the table’s step indicator. The indicator is guidance; players still resolve their cards.

04

Track the battle

Damage

Physical damage uses the Physical Attack Table. Standard energy attacks cost two stages and deal four life cards; card text can override these.

Anger

At five anger, advance a level, reset anger, maximize stages and discard Drills.

Use the table’s counters and card controls to apply results manually. Check the full reference for Endurance, Dragon Ball damage exceptions, capture conditions and card-specific interactions.

05

Your browser is the playmat

Move cards with a mouse or your finger

Drag one of your cards onto your play area. Drop it where you want it to stay. On a phone or tablet, drag with one finger; move near the upper or lower screen edge to scroll. The other player receives the position when you drop.

You can also tap a card, choose a destination in the Card Inspector, and select Move card.

Inspect, flip and mark a card used

Select any visible card to inspect it. For your cards in play, use Flip to change which side your opponent sees, or Mark used as a reminder. You can still see the identity of your own face-down cards.

Manage the Life Deck and hand

Draw 3 and Draw 1 move cards into your private hand. Search Life Deck opens your private search panel; finish with Finish search & shuffle. The opponent sees that you are searching, but cannot see the search results.

Adjust personality level, anger and stages

Use the plus and minus buttons beside your counters. Changing the MP level does not automatically resolve every associated rule: make the other required adjustments yourself. Ally stages are available through the Card Inspector.

Practice or invite a friend

Solo practice lets you switch between both seats. In a private room, Copy invite gives you a link for your opponent. Both players load decks before the host starts setup.

Resolve a disagreement

Use the game log and chat to explain the action, compare the relevant card text, and agree on the correction before continuing. This tabletop helps track the game; it does not adjudicate every card interaction.

06

Know which rules you’re playing

This is a concise, independently written guide for the original Score Entertainment game, using the Buu Saga rules as its baseline. It is not a complete replacement for advanced rulings, errata or deck-construction requirements.

Agree on an edition and tournament rules before starting. Retro tournament house rules can differ from the printed Buu Saga rules.
