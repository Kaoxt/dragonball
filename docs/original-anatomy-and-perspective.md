# Original anatomy and perspective review

Apply this gate to every new or revised DB OCG illustration in every set and card family. Use the user's original when supplied. Keep separately approved departures from the original as explicit exceptions.

Compare the original illustration window and actual finished card at the same visible width, first at normal card size and then enlarged. Do not compare only two modern redraws. A prompt requesting accuracy, a correct export size, clean vector frame, matching hash or passing site test is not evidence that the drawing matches.

Record these checks separately:

1. Head and face: compare forehead height and width relative to the face, hairline corners, jaw/chin, ear placement, head tilt and facial-feature positions. Preserve foreshortening rather than imposing a generic face.
2. Hair silhouette: compare the main masses, parting, tips, direction and major highlight groups. Do not enlarge a mass or confuse hair with a raised arm, sleeve, scarf or background shape.
3. Expression: compare each eye independently, gaze, eyelid openness, brow tension, mouth corner direction, mouth opening and teeth. A small relaxed smirk must not become a stern frown or a broad smile.
4. Anatomy and overlaps: trace every visible hand through wrist, elbow and shoulder to its body; check finger count, grip and which surfaces pass in front. Do not invent limbs or props to explain uncertain shapes.
5. Composition: compare face, fist and torso sizes relative to illustration width, their positions, camera angle and each edge crop. A taller artwork window permits a minimal coherent edge continuation; it does not permit zooming, stretching, changing pose or adding unrelated scenery.
6. Background: inventory actual clouds, broad streaks, terrain and negative space. Preserve their structure and dominant palette. A muted abstract teal/grey backdrop must not become a bright sky filled with clouds or a dramatic speed-line burst.

When any feature is unclear, inspect the original more closely or obtain a better reference; mark that feature unresolved instead of inventing it or declaring a pass. Correct confirmed mismatches, render beneath the retained native frame, and compare both final exported sizes with the original again. Preserve native typography, panels, symbols, game data and all pixels outside the permitted artwork footprint. For expression-only repairs, follow the smaller localized patch workflow in `facial-expression-fidelity.md`.

Record the exact original, final asset version and the specific visual features reviewed. Track `confirmed mismatch`, `corrected and rechecked`, and `published and verified` independently. Review one card at a time; do not extend one card's pass to a batch or call a whole set visually approved from a contact sheet or technical validation.
