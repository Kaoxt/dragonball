# Ultra Rare live finish

All 17 current Ultra Rare cards receive the approved brushed reflective material in the enlarged Cards, Decks and Arena viewers. The material is ported from the final Super Saiyan Goku #125 preview (preview commit `65809cece13abb24b28eddb942baeef41febb429`). It retains the 1.8× diagonal brush strength, original-palette reflection, dark-to-light contrast and natural aura edges. No rainbow sweep, embossed aura rim, shards, dragons or gold frame recolouring is added.

The shared `card-surface.js` resolves only catalog-owned, same-origin image and download paths, including legacy `.jpg` paths and old query strings. It selects Ultra Rare strictly from rarity metadata. Saiyan cards 247–250 now have the missing `Ultra Rare` label, confirmed by their existing `source` URLs and the six-star `verified_game_data.rarity_stars` in `tools/card-corrections/v3-saiyan-221-250-promos/audit.json`. Other rarity data and all original artwork/download files are unchanged. Existing Hi-Tech and standard finishes stay independent.

`ultra-rare-foil.js` renders one 530×742 canvas only after an Ultra Rare is selected. It samples source/texture once per load, caps changing reflections at approximately 30 updates per second, and draws the last pointer position even if input stops during the throttle interval. It follows the shared touch/keyboard/reduced-motion handling. Switching cards or to a hidden back clears the canvas and cancels work; stale image-load callbacks cannot paint the previous card. Image/canvas failures fall back to the original raster. This same canvas path avoids the legacy Hi-Tech gradient/blend stack in Firefox.

`card-effects/ultra-rare/` contains the retained brush material and native-coordinate, softly feathered character masks. Rules panels are excluded; printed white frame text is protected per pixel. Black title, stage and footer bars reflect. Goku retains his approved exact subject silhouette. `ultra-rare-profiles.js` pins each subject mask to its current image revision: changed artwork or future Ultra Rares use the appropriate rules-only fallback until a matching character mask is reviewed. Never transfer Goku's silhouette to another card or add a rectangular exclusion behind HERO.

Validation: catalog coverage for all five sets, legacy/current/download URLs, external/face-down exclusions, card switching, stale-load protection, rendering cancellation/throttling, reduced motion and shared viewer integration. Browser visual checks use the actual shared production modules. Original card rasters are not modified.

Cell Saga 157–160 now use V3 images with matching reviewed subject masks and V3 rules-panel exclusion. The eight-character user attachment on #157 is preserved; #158 and #160 contours are realigned to the final artwork. Each mask is clipped to the artwork window and pinned to its exact new catalog image revision. Evidence: `tools/card-corrections/v3-cell-151-160/foil-mask-review.json`.
