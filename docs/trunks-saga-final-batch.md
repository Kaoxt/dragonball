# Trunks Saga final main batch

Adds cards 181–200 and promos P1–P10, bringing Trunks Saga to 210 published cards and the complete catalog to 603. Every new card has a 1060 × 1484 display WebP and a 2120 × 2968 lossless download. Twenty personality charts are mapped to the printed stages, with index zero representing the zero stage.

Rules were checked against the supplied Expanded CRD. Specific reconciliations include Trunks 185, King Cold 188, Vegeta 195, Confrontation, Concentration Drill, Blue Battle Drill, and Showdown. The original 573 catalog entries are unchanged.

Goku 192 uses the previously approved Goku Hi-Tech artwork because generating its new illustration was blocked twice. Its original scan prints 1,150,000 above 1,200,000; the printed order is retained, with no card-specific correction found in the supplied CRD.

Android Preview 1–6 are prepared as editable cards separately and are excluded from this publication pending the user's logo review. Their Capsule Corp footer emblem is an independent vector group.

Validation: native SVG typography, footer counts and numbers, 11 personality stages, artwork embedding, exact rules text, export dimensions, and PNG/lossless WebP pixel equality; card-type reference check; website test suite and deployment dry run. The card-browser test now checks the fourth page, all 20 additional numbered cards, and all 10 promo routes.
