# Hi-Tech background portraits

The current direction is to retain secondary background portraits with much larger facial features, following the visual approach of Saiyan Level 1 Goku and Piccolo Hi-Tech cards. Position a readable eye, brow or scouter in the visible background beside the stage track. Avoid small complete heads or disconnected fragments. Inspect the finished card with every power-stage panel at normal size and full resolution. Never change shared stage geometry to accommodate decoration.

Keep each character's distinctive palette and painted strokes. For a flattened illustration, generate a localized background patch and mask it away from the retained foreground character. Preserve the character's exact pose, scale, face, expression, linework, cel shading and antialiased contours. Retain all native panels, values, rules, typography and symbols. Verify zero changed pixels outside the permitted background region in both display and download exports, then inspect the finished cards.

The October 26, 2026 revision restores enlarged background portraits on Saiyan #183 Raditz and Trunks #193 Piccolo, #194 Gohan, #196 Garlic Jr., #197 Spice, #198 Vinegar, #199 Trunks, #200 King Cold. This supersedes the earlier removal direction. Other Hi-Tech cards keep their existing treatment except Trunks #192, whose duplicated foreground is replaced with its own original-card pose.

The original removal remains recorded in `tools/card-corrections/hi-tech-backgrounds-2026-10-26/`. The superseding portrait and Goku revision is recorded in `tools/card-corrections/hi-tech-portraits-and-goku-2026-10-26/`. Each revision includes its own source identity and pixel-comparison records. Apply artwork only to its matching card and native coordinates.

For every personality, identify the original card by set and number before selecting art. Check Super Saiyan state, pose, expression, hand shape and crop against that exact reference. Do not reuse another card's illustration as a substitute without an explicit request. Goku the Protector #166 uses a tight head-and-neck crop of its retained illustration. Goku the Unbeatable #159 requires a visible wrist, cuff and forearm connection distinct from his scarf. Trunks #192 uses its original reaching, open-hand pose and its verified top stages of 1,200,000 and 1,100,000. Check anatomy and visibility again after native panels are applied.

Keep visual review, completed artwork correction, saved source and verified live publication as separate statuses. A review or guideline alone does not fix a card.
