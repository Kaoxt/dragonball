# Site design versions

Version 2 is the default site design. It retains the existing playmat background image and luminosity blend, with a lighter charcoal overlay. Its 56px navigation uses the reviewed card-title brush silhouette, dark fringe and soft shadow. The fill matches the darker Start New Topic brush exactly, including its one-third black overlay and underlying texture. Logo sizing and the existing two-line hamburger are preserved from the approved preview.

The site footer offers **Version 1** and **Version 2**. A choice is saved in this browser and applies across pages. Direct links `/?design=v1` and `/?design=v2` override that preference. Header links also carry the choice when browser storage is disabled.

Version 2 also loads `public/surfaces-design.css`: card power-box frames, card-title brush headings for News and Rules, forum headings with full-width dark card-title texture, clean edges and bottom dividers, aligned forum rows, and neutral striped rulebook tables. The nine-slice `public/assets/power-panel-v2.webp` (2x lossless export of the retained editable SVG) keeps chamfered corners and connected charcoal-metal highlights, with darker rails and corner caps, uneven grain and shallow chips. The reference-matched `public/assets/panel-worn-v2.webp` supplies the mottled charcoal field, quiet center and organic wear around panel edges; one image spans each panel, with no repeated diagonal scratch pattern. Forum rows are transparent over that continuous texture, without alternating fills or hover/focus lightening. Forum section headers reuse the dark card-title fill with a one-third black overlay as an unmasked strip spanning the inner frame; only the background is chamfered at the top corners to preserve the metal caps, and the full-width bottom divider remains. Forum secondary copy uses neutral grey (#b8c0c6), replacing the old pink tint in category descriptions, dates and post counts. Start New Topic uses a compact dark brush silhouette behind its label, a 44px minimum touch target and an unclipped keyboard-focus outline. Main sidebar, forum, account, lobby, deck and rulebook chapter panels retain frames. News stories alone have no panel background or border, omit the category badge, and keep a wider brush behind the title with extra width on both ends, greater on its left and bounded to the page gutter on small screens. Show more/less uses the darker charcoal, link actions use the lighter charcoal, and both share compact 8px corners, 44px minimum height, neutral grey hover/focus states and wrapping mobile spacing. Load more follows the same button treatment. News previews continue across paragraph breaks and show up to three lines; only genuinely overflowing text receives an ellipsis. The right sidebar uses regular, matching typography for Your Next Move and Community; their centered textured heading strips meet the inner top rail with the brush ends hidden. Sidebar link rows span the inner frame with horizontal dividers only; background-only chamfers protect the lower corner caps. All sidebar links, including Enter the Arena, use the same dark resting fill (#1b2126); their shared hover fill (#303940) reuses the former Arena highlight. Forum post-header and author-inset paint follows the same angled corners without clipping action menus. The legacy heading image remains available in Version 1. Header and sidebar navigation use charcoal fills and grey accents, including selected, hover and keyboard-focus states. Smaller controls and nested tables keep quieter borders. The border does not clip content or post-action menus and does not change the 56px navigation. Calculator selections retain their high-contrast highlights and wide tables keep horizontal scrolling. The account name has a transparent resting background and a dark grey hover state. All companion surface rules are screen-only and scoped to Version 2; Version 1 and printed rules retain their original styles.

Texture asset provenance: generated with the built-in Image Generation tool from the user-approved forum preview on 2026-10-29, then encoded as WebP for delivery. Prompt: extract only the panel interior; remove all text, icons, dividers, brush, frame and caps; retain very dark cool charcoal mottling, a quiet center, and fine irregular branching cracks and worn grain concentrated at the perimeter. Avoid repeated diagonal scratches or artificial hatching.

News and Forums responsiveness: Version 2 paints the playmat on one fixed viewport-sized composited background layer instead of rescaling a document-sized blended image during article expansion or route changes. Distressed frame grain is baked into a 25,100-byte WebP rather than generated by live SVG turbulence filters. The panel texture remains a shared 33,818-byte WebP. News Show more/less moves the existing lead-image node into its original full-article position and back; it no longer swaps duplicate images. Same-page forum/news links (including Back links without a hash or design query) use the hash router rather than reloading the document. Route GET responses may be reused in memory for 15 seconds, at most eight entries, with concurrent-request deduplication and cloned results. Writes and auth changes invalidate reuse; inbox responses are excluded and HTTP requests retain no-store. Old route content keeps its layout until the next response is ready. The navigation remains sticky and 56px high.

The preceding Version 2 panel style is saved at commit `4125c31a4a08f388baba10468aa166c0e107b6c0` if the power-box experiment needs to be reverted independently of the navigation.

Version 1 remains in `public/navigation.css` and the original background styles. Those files are unchanged. Every new design rule is scoped to `html[data-site-design=v2]`, so choosing Version 1 immediately restores the original header and background without replacing any source or account/game data.

Before Version 2: commit `15aff432b55d8b8962670abebda5f219a59f4d2d`. To restore Version 1 as the default for new visitors, change the fallback in `public/navigation-design.js` from `v2` to `v1`. Existing browser preferences continue to work.
