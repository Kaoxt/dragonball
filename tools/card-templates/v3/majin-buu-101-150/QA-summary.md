# Production and QA — Majin Buu Saga 101–150

## Result

46 finished cards; four explicitly blocked illustrations (#116, #117, #131, #146). All 50 layouts passed the independent technical verifier. Every full-card layout was visually reviewed; the finished cards also received 100%-scale export detail review. This is a visual inspection, not a claim that generated art is a pixel-for-pixel copy of the original scans.

44 new selected illustrations were created. Cards #121 and #122 reuse the approved alternate Dende summoning artwork and placement. Native V3 components supply all titles, category labels, powers, icons, stage values, rarity symbols and footers. Card text was not generated in the illustrations.

## Source and wording

The original Score scans are included with source URLs in metadata. Machine OCR is retained verbatim only as provenance; garbled OCR was checked against the scans. The supplied Expanded CRD was consulted before modern wording.

| Card | Applied ruling |
|---|---|
| 104 Saiyan Hurricane Kick | Discard-to-retain payment is allowed only on its first use. |
| 114 Majin Buu Level 4 | The eight-card shuffle is an activated Power. No infinity icon. |
| 134 Losing Battle | Applies to opponent's non-Personality/non-Mastery cards containing win or wins for the rest of the turn; cannot be stopped by Trunks Energy Sphere. |
| 145 Saiyan Pressure Technique | Two Sensei Deck cards may be Banished only once per physical attack. |
| 150 The Eternal Dragon's Quest | Only one copy may be used per game; your card powers cannot end Combat for the remainder of the game. |

Combat Event becomes Event; Non-Combat becomes Setup, with Drills kept distinct. PAT becomes AT; remove-from-game wording becomes Banish. Deck limits precede the effect. Damage formulas and attack/block/constant icons follow the established conventions. Additional numerical costs, restrictions and effects were not invented to fill space. Energy attacks retain the standard two-stage cost where applicable.

#118 includes both Setups and Drills in the original Non-Combat scope. #135 affects Setups, excluding Drills. #138 Elder Kai Sensei has capacity 21 and no printed power: its blank rules panel is intentional. #146 retains the Rejuvenation timing of its secondary effect. #150 is a Setup, despite an inconsistent source-product category tag.

## Templates and artwork

The Mastery donor was recovered from complete CRC-verified ZIP entries and matched the approved SVG SHA-256 `10948151b2cb82fa190ec88cdf52e8ab8db469acf4c3c7baaf13096f1a17a78c`. The Sensei source matched approved SHA-256 `f9241191ab02217c7acda986c568e552c3c93a8a3bf944e5b437e4d36cf28243`. Retained template geometry was used for these families. Style-specific Mastery colors and existing thin style emblems are applied natively.

#125's small playing-card ranks were corrected. #130's generated empty card boxes were removed from the illustration before assembly. #142's four figures were moved into the upper composition to preserve them inside the pointed Mastery crop. #147's glowing eyes and #149's fusion contact are intentional source features.

All ten personalities have eleven stage entries, including a single bottom zero. #114 uses 7,060,000 for its lowest nonzero stage as printed. Title, level and PUR values were checked against the sources. Dende balls #121 and #122 have six and seven upright stars respectively.

## Image sizes and checks

Portrait display: 1060×1484. Portrait download: 2120×2968. Landscape cards #110 and #113: 1484×1060 display and 2968×2120 download. Both WebP sizes use lossless encoding. Full-size WebP pixels match the corresponding SVG-rendered PNG intermediates.

Illustration source dimensions are recorded per card. Most combat illustrations were generated around 1517×1037 and are scaled within the native art window for export; a 2120×2968 card export does not imply that its illustration was generated at that full resolution. The original #150 reference is only 260×368; it was used as a composition guide, with new artwork rather than an enlarged scan for the finished card.

The verifier checks relative asset resolution, duplicate SVG IDs, absence of live SVG text, native text/metadata agreement, row limits and font size, stage/PUR/level values, Dragon Ball star counts, Sensei capacity, Mastery style emblems, pending-card labels, footer second-pass stability, power-icon alignment, dimensions, SHA-256 hashes, and lossless pixel equality. All 50 passed. The four pending illustrations remain blocked regardless of these layout checks.
