## Production

### Artwork-edge check for every Saiyan Saga card

Before export, inspect the entire illustration perimeter at full resolution and normal card size. Reject unintended black strips, letterboxing or exposed backing above or around the artwork, including the issue flagged on the Power Up and Vegeta previews. Check Freestyle cards especially carefully for the recurring thick black strip directly above the character artwork; this is not part of the approved frame. Retain the approved thin silver border and its narrow dark outline; do not confuse natural dark scene details with padding.

Check source padding, aspect-ratio fitting, clip paths and backing layers. Preserve the original camera distance, subject scale and full composition. Never hide a gap by zooming, cropping to fill, stretching the illustration, thickening the frame or moving shared panels. If the source aspect ratio leaves empty space, prepare a seamless continuation of the scene at the edges through the illustration workflow while preserving the original view, then recheck the exported PNG and WebP. Do not export a card while an unintended strip remains. This requirement applies to upcoming production; the user deferred fixing the current previews.

### Workflow

1. Inspect the actual original card illustration and the retained artwork reference before modernizing a card. Reuse an already approved modern illustration if it meets this standard; do not repeatedly repaint it for frame or typography edits.
2. When modernization is needed, follow the current imagegen skill and edit/generate the illustration separately, using the original scene and this style reference. Request clean artwork only, at sufficient resolution for the final artwork window. Exclude card text, frames, badges, numbers and watermarks from the generated artwork.
3. Retain source art alongside its modernized replacement. Fit the artwork without distortion and protect faces, hands and essential action from crops. Composite it under the approved native SVG layout. Keep titles, powers, borders, numbers, stars and UI icons as native geometry or font outlines; do not regenerate a complete card to obtain new art.
4. Inspect every rebuilt card at full size and normal card size for facial likeness, anatomy, clean contours, coherent light/shadow, crop, embedded old UI and consistency with the reference. Keep all game data, CRD corrections and the accepted Toku Waza/Freestyle colors and symbols independent of the artwork update.
5. Export lossless masters and clearly identify the illustration as raster. A clean illustrated image is not automatically vector artwork. Keep the old approved masters intact. Report which cards were actually rebuilt; saving this direction does not mean the existing set or website images have already been updated.

For artwork modernization, this instruction supersedes older blanket directions to retain original illustration pixels unchanged. For formatting-only edits, continue to preserve approved illustration pixels. Website publication remains a separate requested action.
