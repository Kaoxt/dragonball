# Black Style palette

Approved October 10, 2026, using the user's Black Erasing Drill reference.

- Black Style uses charcoal `#292c30` for title panels and colored frame accents.
- Named/Freestyle cards retain their existing lighter neutral gray (`#8d959d` in the current vector template).
- Keep white lettering and the clean silver vector borders. Do not darken illustrations, change game text, or recolor Named/Freestyle cards.
- Apply this distinction to every future Black Style card and every replacement export, including drills.
- The 18 existing Black Style cards are recorded in `docs/black-style-charcoal.json`. The legacy #235 export receives the same dark title treatment while retaining its existing silver ornamentation until its full frame modernization.
- Updated rendering palettes must remain distinct; do not reuse the Freestyle palette for Black Style.
