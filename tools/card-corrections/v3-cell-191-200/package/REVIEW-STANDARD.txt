REQUIRED EXPRESSION AND FIT REVIEW — ALL CARD FAMILIES

Compare the original reference with the actual finished card, not just the generated art.
Check each eye independently: opening, pupil, gaze, eyelid and brow tension.
Check mouth opening, teeth or tongue visibility, raised/lowered corners and smile versus shout/frown.
Keep head tilt, face proportions, original pose and clothing. Do not replace a distinctive expression with a generic stern face.
Inspect visible hands, finger count, wrists and limb connections at enlarged size.
Check line continuity at hair tips, jaw, fingers, cloth seams and armor; remove accidental doubled or wavy outlines.
Judge character fit in the usable space left of the stage column; check face, hand, shoulder and torso clearance in the final card.
Record unavoidable original-style crops and overlaps. Never label unclear reference details a verified match.
Retain native text, panels, values and symbols during artwork changes.
Record the exact reference and final artwork/export hashes, review each card separately, and distinguish corrected/saved from published/verified.
